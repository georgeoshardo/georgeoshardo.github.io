Interactive materials for the prokaryotic transcription lecture of Molecular Biology of Genes (BIOM335). Both are best viewed on a laptop or desktop.

: An interactive 3D animation of the bacterial transcription cycle, from promoter binding to termination, built from published RNA polymerase structures.

[Proteins of bacterial transcription <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span>]({{ '/teaching/biom335/2-prokaryotic-transcription/protein_browser/' | relative_url }})
